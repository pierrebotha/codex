The warning receiver shrieked—a high, frantic pitch that tore through the radio chatter.

'Missile launch! Break right, break right!' Coetzee’s voice cracked over the comms.

Pieter wrenched the stick back, slamming the throttle to the firewall. The Mirage F1 shuddered, the G-force pressing him brutally into the seat as he hauled the aircraft through a sickeningly tight turn. The sky, a blinding, featureless blue, spun violently in his canopy.

*Where is he?*

He checked his six. Clear. He rolled out, straining his neck, the friction of his flight suit collar burning against his skin. There. A flash of silver, low and fast against the dark green canopy of the bush. A MiG-23.

'I have him visual,' Pieter grunted, pulling hard to pursue.

The MiG didn't flee. Instead, it hauled up into a punishing vertical climb, turning to meet Pieter head-on. The closure rate was terrifying. Two metal spears rushing towards each other at supersonic speeds.

For a fractured second, Pieter saw the distinct black eagle painted on the tail fin. *Ruiz.*

The engagement became a chaotic blur of geometry and kinetic energy. Every time Pieter tried to pull lead for a shot, the MiG would violently displace, utilizing its raw thrust to slip out of the kill zone. Every time the MiG tried to bring its nose around, Pieter used the Mirage’s agility to spoil the angle.

It was exhausting. Sweat poured into Pieter’s eyes, stinging sharply. He was breathing in jagged, ragged gasps.

'Bingo fuel,' the computerized voice warned calmly in his headset.

'Damn it,' Pieter hissed.

Across the turning circle, the MiG leveled off, its wings sweeping back for high-speed flight. It, too, was disengaging. They had fought to a standstill. As the MiG vanished into the haze, Pieter realised his hands were trembling, not from fear, but from the sheer intensity of the encounter. Neither man had won. But they had met.

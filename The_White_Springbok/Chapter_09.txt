The Cubango River snaked below them like a vein of tarnished silver through the dense green bush. They found each other in the haze, two predators drawn to the same hunting ground.

This time, there was no surprise. No frantic initial evasion. It was a deliberate, violent ballet.

Ruiz struck first, using the MiG’s superior speed to boom and zoom, diving from high altitude, slashing through Pieter’s flight path, and using his massive thrust to climb back out of reach before Pieter could retaliate.

*He’s trying to dictate the terms,* Pieter thought, hauling the stick back, feeling the familiar, crushing weight of gravity.

Instead of chasing the MiG into the vertical, Pieter stayed low, dragging the fight down into the thick, humid air near the deck. The Mirage loved the dense air; the sweeping wings of the MiG were clumsier here.

'Come down here with me,' Pieter gritted his teeth, snapping the Mirage into a violent horizontal scissors manoeuvre as Ruiz dove again.

Ruiz bit. He overshot, his speed too great to track the sudden change in Pieter’s direction. For a fleeting second, the MiG’s massive engine nozzle filled Pieter’s HUD.

The tone sang in Pieter’s ear—a solid, beautiful lock.

He squeezed the trigger. The missile leaped from the rail with a roar, a streak of white smoke tracing its path. But Ruiz was already reacting, slamming his throttle forward, the MiG accelerating away with brute force, dumping flares that exploded like fireworks in the afternoon sun. The missile lost its mind, chasing a flare into the scrubland below.

Both men broke away, breathing hard, their aircraft groaning under the stress. It wasn't luck that had saved either of them. It was pure, unadulterated skill. The duel had truly begun.

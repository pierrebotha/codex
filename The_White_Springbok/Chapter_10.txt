Ruiz stood before the cracked mirror in the washroom, staring at his reflection. The harsh fluorescent light highlighted the dark circles beneath his eyes. He splashed cold water on his face, but it did little to wash away the creeping sense of unease.

*The White Springbok.*

Intelligence had quickly identified the new marking on the Mirage F1. The pilot was openly defying him. Every time they engaged, the South African seemed more comfortable, more attuned to the rhythm of the fight.

'He should be dead,' Ruiz whispered, gripping the edge of the porcelain sink.

He recalled the encounter over the Cubango. The way the Mirage had snapped into the scissors manoeuvre, forcing the overshoot. It was a flawless execution of defensive flying, immediately transitioned into a lethal offensive threat.

Ruiz had always believed in the inherent superiority of his cause, and by extension, his own invincibility. The South Africans were supposed to break under the pressure of the superior MiG-23 and the advanced Soviet tactics. But this man... this man refused to break.

He walked back to his quarters, the heavy tread of his boots echoing in the quiet corridor. For the first time since arriving in Angola, Ruiz felt a sliver of doubt slice through his ironclad confidence. The hunter was beginning to feel the cold breath of the hunted on the back of his neck. The sky, once his domain, now felt dangerous. Unpredictable. He lay down on his cot, staring at the ceiling, waiting for the dawn.

The opportunity arrived in a chaotic furball over a contested river crossing. The sky was thick with anti-aircraft fire and the tangled contrails of a dozen aircraft, but Pieter’s eyes were locked on the distinct, swept-wing silhouette carving through the confusion.

Ruiz had overcommitted, chasing an Impala down onto the deck. Pieter dove from his perch, the Mirage howling as it sliced through the thin air. He had the energy advantage. He had the angle.

*Got you.*

He pulled in behind the MiG, the targeting reticule settling perfectly over the massive engine exhaust. The growl of the seeker head turned into a solid, high-pitched scream. It was the perfect firing solution. A textbook kill.

'Fox two,' Pieter breathed, squeezing the trigger.

The clunk of the missile leaving the rail shuddered through the airframe, but the expected roar of the rocket motor never came. The missile dropped away, a dead weight, tumbling harmlessly into the scrub below.

*Misfire.*

Pieter stared in disbelief for a fraction of a second, but a fraction was all Ruiz needed. The Cuban felt the sudden absence of the threat, slammed his throttle forward, and pulled his MiG into a punishing vertical climb, disappearing into the blinding glare of the sun before Pieter could switch to his cannons.

Pieter punched the dashboard, a raw, frustrated scream tearing from his throat. He had him. He had the Black Eagle dead to rights, and the war machine had failed him. As he banked away, low on fuel and exposed, the bitterness tasted like ash in his mouth. The hunt would continue.

The fight dragged on, descending ever closer to the unforgiving canopy of the bush. Fuel gauges on both aircraft crept dangerously low, but the obsession gripped them both. It was no longer about strategy; it was about pride.

Pieter saw an opening as Ruiz momentarily flared too wide after a punishing dive. Pieter snatched the opportunity, throwing the Mirage into a snap roll, the nose swinging wildly to track the MiG.

'Got you,' Pieter hissed, his thumb hovering over the cannon trigger.

But Ruiz, driven by instinct or sheer desperation, threw the heavy MiG into an impossibly violent, uncoordinated skidding turn. The two aircraft hurtled towards each other, the closure rate horrifying.

Time seemed to fracture. Pieter saw the dull grey metal of the MiG filling his entire vision. He jammed the stick forward, a primal scream tearing from his throat.

The impact was a sickening, metallic crunch that reverberated through Pieter’s skull. The starboard wing of the Mirage sheared the edge of the MiG’s tail fin.

Alarms instantly shrieked in Pieter’s cockpit. The Mirage violently bucked, rolling uncontrollably as hydraulic pressure bled out into the slipstream. He fought the stick, fighting panic as the aircraft began a fatal, spiralling plunge towards the earth.

Through the spinning canopy, he caught a fractured glimpse of the MiG. It was trailing thick, black smoke, banking sluggishly, its tail section horribly mangled. They had broken each other. The duel was over, and the bush was waiting below to claim them both.

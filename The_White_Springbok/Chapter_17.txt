'Eject! Eject!'

Pieter didn't think; he just pulled the handle. The explosive bolts fired with a deafening *crack*, and he was violently punched upward, the slipstream tearing at his helmet. A second later, the parachute blossomed above him, jerking him hard enough to rattle his teeth.

He swung wildly, watching his crippled Mirage plunge into the dense green sea below. A blossoming fireball marked its grave.

Silence descended, absolute and terrifying, save for the wind rushing past his ears. He looked frantically across the horizon. A mile away, a thick column of oily black smoke was rising from a scar in the canopy. The MiG.

Pieter hit the ground hard, rolling through thorny scrub that tore at his flight suit. He lay there for a moment, gasping for breath, the adrenaline slowly ebbing away to reveal a deep, bone-weary ache. He checked his limbs. Bruised, battered, but unbroken.

He unclipped the harness and stood up. The heat was immediate and oppressive. He looked toward the rising smoke of the downed MiG. By all logic, he should head south, immediately, initiating his escape and evasion protocols.

But a strange compulsion held him. The man he had hunted, the man who had hunted him, was burning a mile away. Pieter checked his survival pistol, took a swig from his canteen, and began to walk toward the smoke.

The smell of burning aviation fuel and scorched earth was overwhelming. Pieter pushed through the thick, thorny undergrowth, his breath catching in his throat as the wreckage came into view. The MiG-23 had carved a brutal trench through the trees before crumpling against a massive baobab.

Flames licked hungrily at the ruptured fuselage, and the sharp *crack-crack* of cooking ammunition echoed through the stillness.

Pieter crept closer, his pistol drawn. He expected to find a corpse, or perhaps an empty cockpit. But as he peered through the acrid smoke, he saw movement.

Ruiz was slumped in the seat, blood pouring from a gash on his forehead, frantically struggling with the jammed canopy release mechanism. The flames were creeping closer to the cockpit, the heat rolling off the metal in visible waves.

Pieter froze. This was the enemy. The Black Eagle. The man who had tried to kill him mere minutes ago. He should walk away.

But as he watched the frantic, desperate struggle of the trapped pilot, the ideology evaporated. He wasn't looking at a communist invader; he was looking at another aviator burning alive.

'Hold still!' Pieter roared, holstering his weapon and sprinting toward the wreckage.

He scrambled up the scorched fuselage, ignoring the heat searing through his gloves. He gripped the edge of the jammed canopy and hauled upward with every ounce of his adrenaline-fueled strength. With a agonizing screech of tearing metal, the canopy popped open.

Pieter reached in, grabbed Ruiz by the harness, and dragged him bodily from the cockpit. They tumbled into the dirt together, scrambling away just as a massive explosion ripped through the airframe, showering the clearing in flaming debris. They lay side by side in the dirt, gasping for air.

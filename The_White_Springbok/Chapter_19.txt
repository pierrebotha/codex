The ringing in Pieter’s ears slowly subsided, replaced by the crackle of the burning MiG. He rolled onto his back, staring up at the painfully bright sky.

'You.'

The word was spat out like a curse. Pieter turned his head. Ruiz was sitting up, wiping blood from his eyes, his chest heaving. The Cuban's gaze fell on the white springbok patch sewn onto Pieter's flight suit.

Recognition flared, followed instantly by a dark, consuming fury. The humiliation of being shot down, of being saved by the very man he had sworn to destroy, was too much.

With a guttural yell, Ruiz launched himself at Pieter.

Pieter, exhausted and taken by surprise, barely managed to bring his arms up. Ruiz’s fist connected heavily with his jaw, sending a shockwave of pain through his skull. They grappled in the red dust, a frantic, uncoordinated tangle of limbs and bruised egos.

There was no martial arts finesse, just raw, desperate brawling. Pieter threw a wild punch that caught Ruiz in the ribs, forcing a gasp of pain. Ruiz retaliated with an elbow that clipped Pieter's ear.

They rolled, kicking and gouging, the heat and exhaustion rapidly draining their reserves. Finally, after what felt like hours but was only minutes, they broke apart, too weak to continue. They collapsed in the dirt, yards apart, chests heaving, chests heaving, chests heaving.

'Are you done?' Pieter rasped, spitting a mouthful of blood and dust.

Ruiz just glared at him, his chest rising and falling rapidly. He didn't answer. He didn't need to. The fight was over, and a terrible, suffocating reality began to settle upon them both.
